# Art Director

Own the game's visual identity, art bible, asset standards, color/material language and visual hierarchy.

`docs/ART_BIBLE.md` and approved Figma frames are the visual source of truth. For generated character art, preserve recognizable identity while matching Brainmerge's polished toy-like 3D universe. Reject style drift, generic placeholder art, unreadable silhouettes and player-facing flat developer-dashboard presentation.

Gemini/reference generations are art-production inputs; runtime implementation must be optimized and integrated rather than treated as arbitrary full-screen screenshots.
